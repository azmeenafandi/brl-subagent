# Friction Log

> **Purpose:** capture workflow friction the moment it's noticed — before the
> dopamine of a merged PR flushes the thought. Reviewed at sprint end; each
> entry is a candidate for a framework fix, a doc fix, or acceptance.
>
> **Capture rule:** one line, immediately, no elaboration, no judgment, no
> "we should fix X" — just what happened. If it took three steps, say three
> steps. Trivial entries welcome; they're the ones that compound.
>
> **Format:** `YYYY-MM-DD | <tag> | <observation>`
> Tags are freeform — worktree, docs, tooling, process, coordination — pick
> whatever fits. This is a log, not a schema.
